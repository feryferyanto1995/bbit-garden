# Product

## Register

brand

## Platform

web

## Users

Peers, fellow designers, and the curious internet — people who arrive from an Instagram bio link or a shared URL and stay because the page itself is interesting. Secondary: anyone who might later want to work with Fery; the site should quietly prove craft without pitching.

## Product Purpose

bbit is Fery's personal brand playground: a home for UX work, photography (@blancc.canvas), and the "bibit" philosophy — in Bahasa Indonesia, *bibit* means seed. The site exists to be an expression of that philosophy: a small, patient, living thing on the internet. Success is a visitor slowing down, playing with the garden, and remembering the feeling.

## Positioning

A portfolio that practices its own philosophy — it grows while you read it.

## Conversion & proof

- Primary CTA: none in the transactional sense; the soft ask is "plant a seed" (interact) and follow @blancc.canvas / email thebibit.lab@gmail.com.
- Secondary: view project case studies (placeholder content for now).
- The line a visitor remembers: "bibit means seed — just start anyway."
- Belief ladder: (1) this person cares about small details → (2) the philosophy is genuine, not decoration → (3) I want to see what they make next.
- Proof on hand: the site itself, plus real photography in `public/images/photo-1..7.jpg`.

## Brand Personality

Quiet, precise, poetic. A field guide written by a gentle machine: bookish serif voice, pixel-plant accents, unhurried motion. Warm but never loud; playful but never wacky. Copy reads like marginalia in a botanist's notebook.

## Anti-references

- Corporate SaaS landing pages: no hero metrics, gradient CTAs, "trusted by" strips.
- Dark moody agency sites: no black-void backgrounds, giant white type, cursor blobs.
- Retro-game gimmick: pixel identity stays sophisticated — no arcade fonts (Press Start 2P), no chiptune theming.
- Dribbble-template portfolios: no identical card grids of mockups.

## Design Principles

1. **Practice what you preach** — the seed philosophy must be enacted by the interface (things grow, the garden remembers), not just stated.
2. **Quiet wildness** — ambition goes into depth and craft of interaction, never into loudness.
3. **One idea per fold** — long scroll, deliberate pacing, generous whitespace.
4. **Pixel as punctuation** — pixel elements are accents (wordmark, plants, tiny labels), never the whole costume.
5. **Every wild moment has a calm equivalent** — reduced-motion users get a complete, beautiful site.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Solid defaults: WCAG AA contrast (≥4.5:1 body), full keyboard navigation, visible focus states, `prefers-reduced-motion` alternatives for every animation, semantic HTML, alt text with voice.
